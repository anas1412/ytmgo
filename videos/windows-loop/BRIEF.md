## Intent

Seamless, silent, autoplaying loop for the docs site's Download section,
beside the terminal loop (videos/hero-loop), and built as its pair: the
same `>_y` mark opens into the window at 0.7 s and takes it back at 10.4 s,
on the same beats, so the two read as one family.

Inside, the real Windows app: frames are screenshots of the GUI itself
(cmd/ytmgo-gui/frontend), captured at the window's size from the browser
harness with stub data, and animated on top: the cursor, the typed search,
the progress bar and its time.

## Beats

0.0 mark · 0.95 window opens on Home, a song playing · 2.35 click search ·
2.45 types "daft punk" · 3.35 results · 4.75 double-click "Harder, Better,
Faster, Stronger", it plays · 6.3 click its album, Discovery · 8.1 heart
"Aerodynamic" · 10.4 window folds back · 11 mark.
